Getting around: walk, take GRETA's ferry between river towns (Stillbrook, Lanterncross, Reedmouth),
or FLY with a Flying-type Pokémon to any visited town.

## Levels

- Gym level for the next badge: `12 + 6 × badges`. Leaders' aces sit at that level.
- Wild Pokémon: 6–8 below. Route trainers: about 4 below.
- Elite Four 56–60, Champion 62.

## Cast

Flowkeepers: founder August Reeve (old fisher, Reedmouth), radical lieutenant Vesper, grunts.
Dam company: Director Halvard Crest, chief engineer Ingrid Marlow (Grandma's old colleague).
Jasper's mum Rosalind runs the Pension in Lanterncross. Champion Solenne.

## Story beats

Main events fire when entering the hub with enough badges:

| Badges | Where | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Stillbrook | Power line sabotaged, lights out, Flowkeepers blamed. Rival battle 2. |
| 4 | Weirhaven | Flowkeepers protest at the dam. Reeve speaks. Jasper sides with the company. Rival battle 3. |
| 5 | Home | The family Pokémon gives Beryl an egg (*placeholder species*). |
| 6 | Home | Parents worried: job cuts, inner dam banned. Jasper unsure. Rival battle 4. |
| 7 | Weirhaven | Reservoir rises, ghosts swarm, Lethys in Mirrormere. Vesper plants explosives in the dam; battle. Torvald returns to Stillbrook. |
| 8 | Dam crest | The truth. Crest admits the sealed soul gate. The choice: break / seal / reopen the gate (3 of 4 clues). Final battle on the dam wall. |

Local events: the Weirhaven visit (Ingrid, Crest), the Lanterncross festival and mural (clue 3),
Valecrown Flowkeeper raid (Vesper), Reedmouth (meet Reeve), Ingrid's pump station quest (dam plans,
clue 2, 3+ badges), Lowbank Ruins letters (clue 1, 5+ badges), the hidden maintenance tunnel
(clue 4, 5+ badges), Emberdeep fossils (revived at the Frostspring spring), Pension runaways (EXP Share).

Endings: break the dam, keep it sealed, or open the soul gate. Jasper takes the opposite side
(break vs seal); with the gate he stands with Beryl and the last battle is against Vesper.
The League opens after the choice. Lethys: catchable at the source after the Frostspring badge
(break, seal) or at the delta (gate).
